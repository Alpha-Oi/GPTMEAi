# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

GPTMEAi welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds,
experience levels, identities, and viewpoints. Discussion should help contributors
understand the scenario, the evidence, the tradeoffs, and the actual behavior of the
project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas, instructions, and evidence rather than people.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Keep reports focused on reproducible behavior and the scope of this repository.
- Respect privacy, licensing, and responsible disclosure.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately introducing malicious instructions, credentials, or deceptive artifacts.
- Disruptive repetition, spam, or sustained off-topic conduct.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces
where someone represents this project or participates in its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an ordinary public concern, open an issue with the minimum information needed.
For a sensitive matter, request a private contact channel through the maintainer's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/Alpha-Oi) without posting sensitive details.
Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict
participation when needed to protect the community and project. Reports will be
reviewed in context, with privacy respected as far as practical.
